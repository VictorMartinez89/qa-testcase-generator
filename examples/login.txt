As a registered customer of an online pharmacy,
I want to log in with my email and password,
so that I can see my prescriptions and order history.

Acceptance criteria:
- Email must be a valid email address.
- Password must be 8 to 64 characters.

- A "Forgot password?" link sends a reset email.
- On success the user lands on /dashboard.
